## The Correction

**There is no tag A-0758 in the CMS State Operations Manual, Appendix A.** In the current edition, Rev. 238 (03-20-2026), the tag sequence runs A-0750, then A-0751, then A-0760. A-0758 is not between them or anywhere else in the document.

Appendix A also contains no pest guidance under any tag: zero occurrences of "pest," "vermin," "rodent," or "insect" in the entire manual.

Hospital pest findings are cited under two tags:

- **A-0701**, which enforces 42 CFR §482.41(a), the physical plant standard.
- **A-0750**, which enforces 42 CFR §482.42(a)(3), the sanitary environment provision.

Those three findings were established by the operator's grep of the Rev. 238 PDF on September 10, 2026, and are consistent with this reference's earlier full-document extraction of Rev. 238 (May 25, 2026), which found no pest vocabulary anywhere in the manual.

If you arrived here with "A-0758" written on a citation, a checklist, a vendor report, or a consultant's template, the number is wrong. The rest of this page is what the record actually says, and how to find the tag you were cited under.

## A-0701 — Standard: Buildings (42 CFR §482.41(a))

Verbatim, as reproduced on this reference's [Conditions of Participation page](/authorities/cms-conditions-of-participation/) from eCFR:

> (a) **Standard: Buildings.** The condition of the physical plant and the overall hospital environment must be developed and maintained in such a manner that the safety and well-being of patients are assured.

The regulation contains no pest language. Pest activity in a patient-care area, a storage room, or a support space is cited here as a failure to maintain the environment so that patient safety and well-being are assured. Full treatment: [A-0701](/deficiencies/a-0701-buildings/).

## A-0750 — Sanitary Environment (42 CFR §482.42(a)(3))

The only sanitation language in Appendix A, recorded on this reference's [State Operations Manual page](/authorities/cms-state-operations-manual/) from the full-document extraction of Rev. 238:

> "The hospital must provide a sanitary environment to avoid sources and transmission of infections and communicable diseases. There must be an active program for the prevention, control, and investigation of infections and communicable diseases."

Pest control is not named. A pest finding cited to A-0750 is a finding that the environment is not sanitary, or that the active program did not prevent, control, or investigate a source of infection, with pest activity as the source. Full treatment: [A-0750](/deficiencies/a-0750-sanitary-environment/).

## How to Read the Tag on Your Form CMS-2567

The Statement of Deficiencies and Plan of Correction, Form CMS-2567, is the document a State Survey Agency sends after a survey. The left side of each page is the agency's; the right side is yours.

- The **ID Prefix Tag** column on the left carries the tag: the letter prefix (A for hospitals) and the four-digit number, printed as "A 0701" or "A0701." That is the number your plan of correction answers.
- Beneath the tag, the form prints the **regulation text** the tag enforces, then the surveyor's **summary statement of deficiencies**: what was observed, where, and when.
- The right side, headed **Provider's Plan of Correction**, is completed by the hospital opposite each tag, with a **completion date** for each corrective action.

Read the regulation text printed beneath the tag before anything else. It is the regulation, not the tag number, that the plan of correction has to answer; the tag is the index.
