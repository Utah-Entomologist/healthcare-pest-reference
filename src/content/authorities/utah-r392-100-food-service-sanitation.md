## Citation

Utah Administrative Code Title R392 (Health and Human Services, Population Health, Environmental Health), Rule R392-100, "Food Service Sanitation." Read from the Utah Office of Administrative Rules on September 24, 2026. Authorizing law: Utah Code Sections 26B-1-202 and 26B-7-402.

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**R392-100-4(1) Incorporation by Reference:**

> "(1) The department incorporates by reference the following: (a) Section 402 of the Federal Food, Drug and Cosmetic Act, 21 U.S.C. 342; and (b) the 2022 version of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration Food Code (Food Code), Chapters 1 through 8, Annex 1 Parts 8-6 through 8-9 and the December 2024 version of the Supplement to the 2022 Food Code, with the stated exceptions and amendments as established in Subsections R392-100-4(2) and R392-100-4(3)."

**R392-100-4(2)**, the Food Code provisions Utah does not incorporate:

> "(2) The following provisions of the Food Code are not incorporated into this rule: (a) Paragraph 5-203.15(B); (b) Paragraphs 5-402.11(B), (C) and (D); (c) Section 8-302.14; (d) Paragraph 8-304.11(K); (e) Annex 1, Section 8-909-20; and (f) Annex 1, Paragraph 8-911.10(B)."

**R392-100-3(1)**, the establishments exempt from the rule:

> "(1) The following food service establishments are exempt from the requirements of this rule: (a) a mobile food business; (b) a certified or licensed child care facility, including a residence, that provides care for 16 or fewer children; (c) a residential treatment program, residential support program, or recovery residence, as defined in this rule and in Rule R392-110, that provides a 24-hour group living environment for between four and 16 individuals unrelated to the owner or provider; (d) an agritourism food establishment; and (e) a microenterprise home kitchen."

**The Food Code definition R392-100 incorporates** (FDA Food Code 2022, §1-201.10(B), "Food establishment," paragraph (1)(a)):

> "stores, prepares, packages, serves, vends FOOD directly to the CONSUMER, or otherwise provides FOOD for human consumption such as a restaurant; satellite or catered feeding location; catering operation if the operation provides FOOD directly to a CONSUMER or to a conveyance used to transport people; market; vending location; conveyance used to transport people; institution; or FOOD bank"

## What It Means in Plain Language

R392-100 makes the 2022 FDA Food Code Utah law for food service, with a short list of exceptions. Section 6-501.111, the Food Code's pest control paragraph, is not on the list of provisions Utah leaves out, and R392-100-4(3) amends a neighboring section (6-501.115, live animals) but not 6-501.111. The pest control requirement comes into Utah law as FDA wrote it.

The exemption list in R392-100-3(1) names five kinds of operations. None is a jail or prison. The Food Code definition the rule incorporates names "institution" among the operations that are food establishments.

For a Utah secure facility housing state inmates, UDC Standard G-05 adds the inspection: the health authority having jurisdiction inspects the food service area at least once a year. See the related page.

## Who It Applies To

Food service establishments in Utah, other than the five exempt categories in R392-100-3(1). How a particular local health department permits and inspects a particular jail or prison kitchen is that department's determination and is not asserted here.

## Documentation Evidence

- The local health department's inspection reports for the facility's kitchen, including any pest control findings under Food Code Section 6-501.111
- Corrective actions taken on those findings
- Pest control service records showing each element of Section 6-501.111 was met (see the Food Code page)
