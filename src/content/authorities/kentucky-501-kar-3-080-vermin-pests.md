## Citation

Kentucky Administrative Regulations, Title 501, Chapter 3 (Full-service jails), 501 KAR 3:080, Section 1(1). Read from the Legislative Research Commission's regulation page and its engrossed PDF on September 26, 2026. Last amended effective September 3, 2024 (50 Ky.R. 1954).

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**501 KAR 3:080, Section 1(1):**

> "(1) The jailer or jail administrator shall provide for the control of vermin and pests."

**Section 1(3), preventative maintenance plan:**

> "(3) The jailer or jail administrator shall have a written preventative maintenance plan that includes: (a) A cleaning schedule for various locations and items in the jail; (b) A schedule for inspections by the jailer or jail administrator; (c) A schedule for trash and garbage removal; and (d) A schedule for periodic inspection and maintenance of specific mechanical equipment."

**Necessity and function:**

> "This administrative regulation establishes procedures to provide proper sanitation and hygiene in full-service jails."

## What It Means in Plain Language

1. **The duty is personal to the jailer.** The jailer or jail administrator provides for control of vermin and pests.
2. **No frequency and no provider.** How the jail provides for it is left to the jail.
3. **The maintenance plan is where a schedule would live.** It must include cleaning and inspection schedules, which is the natural home for pest inspections.
4. **Three tiers, same sentence.** Full-service jails (3:080), restricted custody centers (7:080) and life safety jails (13:010) carry near-identical pest text.

## Who It Applies To

Full-service jails, meaning jails that may house state prisoners under KRS 441.055 and meet the Chapter 3 standards. Counties that do not house state prisoners are life safety jails under 501 KAR 13:010, whose Section 8(1) carries the same duty.

**KRS 441.055(1)(a)1.:**

> "Adopt the recommendations of the Jail Standards Commission created pursuant to Executive Order Number 81-1026 and promulgate regulations pursuant to KRS Chapter 13A establishing minimum standards for jails."
