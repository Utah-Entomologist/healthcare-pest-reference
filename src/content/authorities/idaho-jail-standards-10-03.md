## Citation

Idaho Sheriffs' Association, Idaho Jail Standards: Standards for Detention Facilities, Revision April 2024, Standard 10.03, under "Facility Sanitation and Maintenance." Read from the Association's published PDF on September 26, 2026.

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**Standard 10.03:**

> "10.03 The facility has a plan for the control of vermin and pests which includes monthly inspections. If fumigations are necessary, they are done by a licensed pest control professional. (R) (Revised 12/09) (Revised 12/17)"

**How the designations work, as printed:**

> "Following each standard is the designation of mandatory (M) or recommended (R)."

> "The facility must have met 100 percent of the Mandatory Standards and 75 percent of the recommended standards to be eligible for a Certificate of Compliance."

**What the standards are for, as printed:**

> "These Standards are not intended to be used in any legal proceeding to establish a "duty of care" or evidence of a legal duty to any person or entity."

## What It Means in Plain Language

1. **A plan with monthly inspections.** The plan is the jail's; the monthly inspection is part of it.
2. **Licensed professionals for fumigation.** Fumigation is reserved for a licensed pest control professional.
3. **Recommended, not mandatory.** 10.03 carries an (R). A jail can earn a certificate without meeting every recommended standard, but the association's inspection still checks it.
4. **Not a legal duty by its own terms.** The association says the standards are not meant to establish a duty of care. A grievance or claim will still ask what the jail did about the pests.

## Who It Applies To

Idaho full-service jails (Standards for Detention Facilities), as the document describes. The association conducts annual inspections of county jails under its certification process.

## Documentation Evidence

- The written vermin and pest control plan
- Monthly inspection records with dates, locations, findings and actions
- Licensure of any professional who performed fumigation
- The association's annual inspection executive summary
