## Citation

Oregon Revised Statutes 169.076, "Standards for local correctional facilities," subsections (2)(g) and (8), with ORS 169.070 (inspections) and ORS 169.080 (enforcement). Read from the Oregon State Legislature's ORS Chapter 169 page on September 26, 2026.

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**ORS 169.076, lead-in and subsection (2), in part:**

> "Each local correctional facility shall: ... (2) Have a comprehensive written policy with respect to: ... (g) Vermin and communicable disease control."

**ORS 169.076(8):**

> "(8) Ensure that the facility be clean, and provide each confined detainee or prisoner: (a) Materials to maintain personal hygiene. (b) Clean clothing twice weekly. (c) Mattresses and blankets that are clean and fire-retardant."

**ORS 169.070(1), in part (inspections):**

> "The department shall inspect local correctional facilities, lockups, temporary holds and juvenile detention facilities, to ensure compliance with the standards established in ORS 169.076 to 169.078, 169.740 and 419A.059."

**ORS 169.080(2), in part (enforcement):**

> "The provisions of ORS 169.076 to 169.078, 169.740, 419A.059, 419B.160 and 419C.130 shall be enforceable by the Attorney General of the State of Oregon. ... Nothing in this section precludes a private right of suit or action."

## What It Means in Plain Language

1. **A written policy is a statutory duty.** Every Oregon local correctional facility must have a comprehensive written policy on vermin and communicable disease control. Vermin and disease sit in the same line, which fits how jails meet them in practice: lice, scabies mites and bed bugs can all arrive at intake, with people or their belongings.
2. **The facility must be clean.** Subsection (8) is the outcome duty that the policy serves.
3. **Someone checks.** The Department of Corrections inspects against these standards, and the Attorney General can enforce them. The statute says it does not preclude a private action.

## Who It Applies To

Each local correctional facility in Oregon, as ORS 169.076 states. The Department of Corrections' inspection duty under ORS 169.070 covers local correctional facilities, lockups, temporary holds and juvenile detention facilities.

## Documentation Evidence

- The facility's written policy on vermin and communicable disease control
- Intake screening and delousing or treatment records where the policy calls for them
- Pest service and inspection records with dates, locations, findings and actions
- Pest grievances tied to work orders and closure
- Department of Corrections inspection reports and any corrective-measure correspondence under ORS 169.080(1)
