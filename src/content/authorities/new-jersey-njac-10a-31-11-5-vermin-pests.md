## Citation

New Jersey Administrative Code, Title 10A, Chapter 31 (Adult County Correctional Facilities), Subchapter 11 (Sanitation), § 10A:31-11.5. Read from the Department of Corrections' scan of the OAL-certified official pages and matched in the Legal Information Institute's current text on September 26, 2026. Subsection (c) was added by R.2017 d.228, effective December 4, 2017. The chapter was readopted without change as R.2025 d.073, effective May 6, 2025.

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**N.J.A.C. 10A:31-11.5 (the section in full):**

> "(a) Staff at each adult county correctional facility shall make arrangements for the control of vermin and pests.
>
> (b) Licensed pest control professionals shall be used at least once per month to clean or fumigate the facility.
>
> (c) Integrated pest management practices shall be utilized to control the use of toxic pesticides."

**N.J.A.C. 10A:31-11.1:**

> "Each adult county correctional facility shall comply with Federal, State and local sanitation, safety and health codes."

## What It Means in Plain Language

1. **At least monthly.** Licensed professionals service the facility every month at a minimum.
2. **Licensed, not staff.** The service is by licensed pest control professionals; staff make the arrangements.
3. **Integrated pest management is required.** Of the provisions quoted on this reference, New Jersey's is the only jail rule that requires integrated pest management by name, to control the use of toxic pesticides.
4. **Weekly sanitation inspections** by designated staff, using a checklist, are required separately (§10A:31-11.3).

## Who It Applies To

All adult county correctional facilities and facilities or units housing county inmates, under the Department's Office of County Services. The Commissioner's authority to set minimum standards is N.J.S.A. 30:1B-10.

**N.J.A.C. 10A:31-1.2 (Scope):**

> "This chapter shall be applicable to the New Jersey Department of Corrections, the Office of County Services, all adult county correctional facilities and facilities or units housing county inmates."

## Documentation Evidence

- The pest control contract with a licensed professional and the license
- Service records showing at least one visit every month
- The integrated pest management plan: monitoring, thresholds, exclusion and sanitation actions before pesticides
- Pesticide use records showing IPM controlled their use
- Weekly sanitation inspection checklists (§10A:31-11.3)
