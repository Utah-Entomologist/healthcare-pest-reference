## Citation

Virginia Administrative Code, Title 6 (Criminal Justice and Corrections), Agency 15 (State Board of Local and Regional Jails), Chapter 40 (Minimum Standards for Jails and Lockups), Part VI (Jail Physical Plant), Section 6VAC15-40-1150, "Vermin and pest control." Read on the Virginia Legislative Information System on September 24, 2026.

Statutory authority, as printed: §§ 53.1-5, 53.1-68, 53.1-131 and 53.1-133.01 of the Code of Virginia. Historical notes, as printed: derived from VR230-30-001 § 6.5, effective April 1, 1987; last amended effective November 22, 2012 (Virginia Register Volume 29, Issue 4).

## What It Says (Verbatim)

**6VAC15-40-1150. Vermin and pest control** (the section in full):

> "The facility shall control vermin and pests and shall be serviced at least quarterly by a licensed pest control business or personnel certified by the Virginia Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services."

## What It Means in Plain Language

One sentence, two duties:

1. **An outcome.** The facility shall control vermin and pests. The duty is continuous and unqualified.
2. **A floor.** Service at least quarterly, by either a licensed pest control business or personnel certified by the Virginia Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.

Quarterly is the minimum, not the program. A facility with activity between visits still owes the first duty.

**Why it is on a Utah-based reference.** It shows a state writing licensed pest service and a fixed frequency directly into its jail standards. Utah's correctional standards put pest and vermin control on a weekly facility inspection (UDC Standard E-03) but set no service frequency and name no provider qualification.

## Who It Applies To

The section sits in Part VI (Jail Physical Plant) of Virginia's Minimum Standards for Jails and Lockups, adopted by the State Board of Local and Regional Jails. Which facilities the chapter reaches is set in the chapter's own applicability provisions, which are not quoted here.

## Documentation Evidence

- Pest control service records showing at least one service each quarter
- The provider's Virginia pest control business license, or the certification of the personnel performing the work
- Records showing vermin and pests were controlled between services: findings, follow-up, and results
