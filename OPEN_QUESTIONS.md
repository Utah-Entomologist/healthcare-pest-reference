# Open questions — Corrections Pest Reference

Each item stays open until a primary source closes it. No page asserts an answer before then.
The same list is published on /methodology/#open-questions.

## Still open

1. **OSHA coverage of federal employers.** OSHA's Utah State Plan page: "UOSH does not cover
   federal government employers including USPS." How OSHA requirements reach a federal
   detention facility's own staff (OSH Act §19, EO 12196, 29 CFR 1960) is not yet read.
2. **Food code reach outside Utah.** Per state, whether jail and prison kitchens are inspected
   under the adopted food code, and by which agency. Ohio 5120:1-8-05(E) requires an annual
   inspection "by local or state health authorities" for full service jails (on the Ohio page).
3. **BOP.** Current Food Service Manual program statement (P4700.06 vs. P4700.07) and its pest
   provisions; full text of P1600.11 Chapter 3, Section 10 "Pest Control". bop.gov returned a
   404 page to a direct download on 2026-09-24; read through a browser.
4. **Utah county jails holding no state inmates.** What standards they operate under (Utah Code
   Title 17 Ch. 22; Utah Sheriffs' Association jail standards), what is public, and what the
   public text says about pests and sanitation.
5. **General-use pesticides applied by Utah facility staff.** How UDAF reads the first sentence of
   R68-7-9(1)(a) ("Each person performing the physical act of applying a pesticide, shall be
   certified...") for a public employee applying general-use products in the employer's own
   building. Ask UDAF in writing.
6. **USMS.** usmarshals.gov returned 403 to a direct download on 2026-09-24 (Federal Performance-
   Based Detention Standards). Read through a browser.

## Closed 2026-09-24

- **OSHA coverage of Utah public employers.** OSHA stateplans + stateplans/ut; R614-1-4.A.3
  (1910.21 through end of part 1910, July 1, 2025 edition). On the OSHA page.
- **Food code reach in Utah.** R392-100-3(1), R392-100-4(1)-(2); FDA §1-201.10(B) "institution";
  UDC Standard G-05. On the R392-100, FDA, and UDC pages.
- **ICE standards applicability.** PBNDS 2011 1.2 and 4.1 scope sections; NDS 2019 overview
  ("designed for non-dedicated facilities"). On all four ICE pages.
- **Restricted use pesticides applied by Utah facility staff.** R68-7-2(8), R68-7-12(1). On the
  R68-7 page.
